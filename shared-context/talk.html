STYLE: HOW IT WORKS
How the team shares what it knows

Design idea after Honcho by Plastic Labs (honcho.dev): people and agents as peers of one memory

Bridge: that is the picture; this is how we wire it. Sources come in: conversations, meetings and code today; business data next, through a data spine we are building with the data team (today the warehouse's model code is in the graph, not its data). Chat is next, once privacy gates clear. People ask through their own Claude, connected by a plugin; the company bot hands requests to agents (a pilot: much of the work still waits on a person); work that needs an owner's decision waits; results people accept are saved back as notes, and agents' work is linked back through the board. Five presses.
STYLE: OUR GRAPHS
Why a graph
Bridge: why a graph and not a pile of documents? A graph is a map of relationships. Without one the agent searches, reads file after file and fills its context. The structure was already there: each definition a node, each reference an edge. Four presses.

STYLE: KINETIC TYPE
Three tips
Three habits that changed how I work with agents. One press per tip. 1: use the cheaper model for the work and the stronger one as its advisor. 2: let your agent mine your own transcripts and bring you fixes as questions. 3: never take an answer on trust, ask again or have a second model check it.
